# Translucent AI

> Translucent AI is an agentic AI platform that acts as a financial operating system for healthcare organizations, unifying claims, labor, clinical output, P&L, budget/forecast, and contract-economics data to deliver real-time financial visibility and automated root cause analysis for finance and clinical teams. The company raised a $27M Series A led by GV, with backing from NEA, Virtue, FPV, and Redesign Health. It publishes no public API, developer portal, or documentation surface.
